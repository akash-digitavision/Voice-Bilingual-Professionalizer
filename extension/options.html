Keyboard Shortcut

Activate voice recording instantly from any active tab.

Primary Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + V (or ⌘ + Shift + V on Mac)

To change or customize this keybinding in Chrome:
1. Open a new tab and navigate to: chrome://extensions/shortcuts
2. Locate Voice Bilingual Professionalizer.
3. Click the pencil icon and press your desired shortcut key combination.

Privacy & Data Handling

  • Zero Session Scraping: We never inspect, collect, or scrape ChatGPT web cookies or account passwords.
  • Ephemeral Audio: Microphone audio is converted to text in-memory and immediately released. Zero audio is stored.
  • Local Storage: Your API keys are stored in Chrome's sandboxed local storage on your machine.
